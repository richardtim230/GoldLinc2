Admissions Timeline 2025-2026

Important dates for the upcoming academic session. Plan ahead to ensure timely submission of all required documents and participation in all assessment components.

1

Application Window Opens

Date: January 15, 2025

Action: Submit online application form with all required documents including birth certificate, previous academic records, and health reports.

2

Application Deadline

Date: April 30, 2025

Action: Final day to submit all applications. Late submissions will not be accepted. Complete all application fields accurately.

3

Entrance Examinations

Dates: May 17-24, 2025

Details: Written assessments in Mathematics, English Language, and General Reasoning. Tests conducted on campus. Arrival by 8:00 AM for registration.

4

Results & Interview Invitations

Date: May 31, 2025

Action: Results and interview schedules emailed to all applicants. Top performers invited for parent-child interviews.

5

Parent-Child Interviews

Dates: June 2-14, 2025

Details: Interactive sessions with admissions team. Children participate in activities and discussions. Parents discuss educational goals and school expectations.

6

Final Admission Decisions

Date: June 21, 2025

Action: Acceptance/Waitlist notifications sent via email and SMS. Admission offer details and enrollment procedures included.

7

Enrollment Confirmation

Deadline: July 15, 2025

Action: Submit acceptance confirmation form and complete registration. First semester fees and orientation details provided.

Eligibility & Requirements

Primary Section

✓ Grade Levels & Age

  • Nursery 1: Children aged 2.5-3.5 years
  • Nursery 2: Children aged 3.5-4.5 years
  • Basic 1-6: Children aged 5-12 years

✓ Required Documents

  • • Birth Certificate (original & copy)
  • • Previous School Reports (if applicable)
  • • Medical Records & Immunization Certificate
  • • Family Background Form (provided by school)
  • • Passport-sized Photographs (4 copies)

✓ Entrance Assessment

Age-appropriate interactive sessions including observation, storytelling, basic math concepts, and language skills evaluation for Nursery applicants. Formal examinations for Basic 1 and above.

Secondary Section

✓ Grade Levels & Age

  • JSS 1: Students aged 11-13 years
  • JSS 2-3: Students aged 12-15 years
  • SSS 1-3: Students aged 15-18 years

✓ Required Documents

  • • Birth Certificate & National ID
  • • Previous School Transcripts (last 2 years)
  • • SSCE/O'Level Results (if applicable)
  • • Medical & Vaccination Records
  • • Character Reference from Previous School

✓ Entrance Examinations

Comprehensive written tests covering Mathematics, English Language, General Studies, and subjects relevant to intended courses. Duration: 3 hours. Additional oral interview with subject specialists.

Frequently Asked Questions

The application fee is ₦5,000 for all grade levels. This non-refundable fee covers the cost of processing your application and entrance examination. Payment instructions are provided upon form submission.
Mid-session transfers may be considered subject to space availability and completion of the admission process. Interested candidates should contact our admissions office directly for evaluation and scheduling.
School fees vary by grade level and are communicated during the enrollment process. A detailed fee schedule is provided upon acceptance. Payment plans are available for families who require them.
Yes, merit-based and need-based scholarships are available for qualifying students. Details are provided during the admission process. Interested families should contact our admissions office for more information.
The 2025-2026 academic session begins on September 1, 2025. Orientation and registration occur during the week of August 25-29, 2025. Exact dates are provided at enrollment.
